This post will take a look into the state of GUNBOAT-8 and layout the plans for the future of the game.

## The game today 

I released the GUNBOAT-8 demo at the start of October. It's nearly been a month since releasing the demo and the response has been overwhelming.

As of writing, the game has peaked at 55 CCU and seen steady growth week on week since launch. This is crazy for a demo compared to expectations! The demo for my first game, Holding Pattern, peaked at 7 CCU during active development, which game on the second day of Steam Next Fest.

The game has been challenging to market. It has a unique art style that seems only appealing to some. This, I believe, has been a large challenge in getting coverage on the game from press or Youtubers / streamers. I find it even more so incredible how many people have played the game considering it's primarily the result of my own marketing efforts. If you haven't already, ask your favourite creator to checkout the game!

The amount of time some community members have put into the demo is outstanding. People have played for 100s of hours. Endless runs have gone on for 10+ hours. I never expected such a taking to the game's demo. As a result of the feedback received, I have continued to push out new content to the demo in an attempt to bring players back and help show I care for their vision of the game.

To date, the demo has over 100 mods, over 30 weapons, 7 ships and 3 characters. This is approximately half of the content I had planned for the full game. I now worry with how much content has been included into the demo, it will be hard for players to look at the full game for what it can be. There is a risk that players who have invested so much time into the demo have no desire to continue beyond.


## What's next for the game

As a result of the response to the demo, I have gone back and forth over what to do with the game next. I have come to a conclusion on the most appropriate next steps.

- GUNBOAT-8 will release into **Early Access** at the earliest possible opportunity.
- The game will release into early access at **$9.99/£7.50**.
- The demo content will be partially stripped back.

I had originally planned to release the full game in 2026. This is now seemingly more difficult to do. Early Access will give me the platform, as a solo developer, to build with the community and bring the ideas of the community to life. It means **you** can help shape the game. Be that with feedback on how mechanics feel, or ideas for new weapons, ships or mods.

This is already how I have been treating the demo, but it is unsustainable for myself to continue providing this level of support to a free demo. The price point of the game is higher than I had initially intended, but I believe that as the game will receive regular updates through early access, it justifies the price point. I hope that each week, player will return to find new content to play with.

Alongside content, I plan to continue with timed events, such as leaderboards and community goals. I hope these contribute to GUNBOAT-8 being considered a live-service game for the duration of early access

See you on the high seas, Captains.
